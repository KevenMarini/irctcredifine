The Indian Splendour

⏳ 7 Nights / 8 Days
📍 Delhi → Mumbai
👑 From $7,540 / Guest

A regal odyssey traversing the royal heritage of India. Highlights include the Taj Mahal in Agra, tiger safaris at Ranthambore, amber palaces of Jaipur, sandstone forts of Bikaner, blue city of Jodhpur, lake palaces of Udaipur, and the grand Arabian coast of Mumbai.

Heritage of India

⏳ 7 Nights / 8 Days
📍 Mumbai → Delhi
👑 From $7,840 / Guest

Discover the rich tapestry of Indian civilization. From the bustling metropolis of Mumbai to the UNESCO world heritage Ajanta Caves, the royal dining halls of Udaipur, Mehrangarh fort of Jodhpur, and private high tea with the Maharaja of Jaipur.

Treasures of India (Golden Triangle)

⏳ 3 Nights / 4 Days
📍 Delhi → Agra → Jaipur → Delhi
👑 From $3,850 / Guest

The quintessential Indian voyage. Witness sunrise over the pristine marble of the Taj Mahal, venture deep into the wilderness of Ranthambore National Park in search of the Royal Bengal Tiger, and explore the Pink City of Jaipur.

Bharat Gaurav: Spiritual Varanasi & Ayodhya

⏳ 4 Nights / 5 Days
📍 Delhi → Ayodhya → Varanasi → Prayagraj
🕉️ From ₹ 18,950 / Guest

An uplifting spiritual circuit aboard the state-of-the-art Bharat Gaurav Tourist Train. Experience the majestic Ganga Aarti on the holy Dashashwamedh Ghat, visit Kashi Vishwanath corridor, and pay obeisance at the Ram Janmabhoomi temple in Ayodhya.
